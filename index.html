About the chapter

A community of students who care about decisions

INFORMS at Northeastern is a community of graduate and undergraduate students passionate about operations research, data analytics and industrial engineering. Founded in the summer of 2015, the chapter bridges academic theory and practical application through seminars, workshops, research talks and networking.

We are the Northeastern chapter of INFORMS, the Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences, the largest professional society in the field. Membership is free and open to every Northeastern student, in any program.

Bylaws

In preparation by the 2026-27 board.

The chapter's bylaws are being written on the INFORMS model for student chapters. Once adopted by the membership and approved by INFORMS, the full text will appear here. These are the principles under discussion.

Membership
Open to every Northeastern student with an interest in operations research, the management sciences or analytics. INFORMS membership is not required to join.
Officers
A board of elected officers, each a member of INFORMS, serving one-year terms.
Elections
Open nominations and an electronic ballot of the membership, decided by majority vote.
Meetings
General meetings open to all members, announced in advance.
Conduct
Everyone taking part in chapter activities agrees to the INFORMS Code of Conduct.

Join the chapter

Membership is free and open to every Northeastern student: undergraduate, master's or doctoral, in any program. Members receive event announcements and invitations, and can help run the chapter.
