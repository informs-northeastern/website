About the chapter

INFORMS is the main professional society for operations research and analytics. We're its student chapter at Northeastern, started in the summer of 2015.

Operations research is the math behind a lot of everyday decisions: hospital staffing, delivery routes, power grid planning, airline schedules, even how AI models get trained. You don't need to study it to join. Most of what we do is invite people who use it to come and talk about their work, teach practical skills, and give students across programs a reason to meet.

Joining is free, and you don't need to be an INFORMS member.

Bylaws and questions

Bylaws

The 2026-27 board is writing these, based on the INFORMS template for student chapters. The full text goes here once members adopt it and INFORMS approves it. For now, the main points:

Membership
Open to every Northeastern student with an interest in operations research, the management sciences or analytics. INFORMS membership is not required to join.
Officers
A board of elected officers, each a member of INFORMS, serving one-year terms.
Elections
Open nominations and an electronic ballot of the membership, decided by majority vote.
Meetings
General meetings open to all members, announced in advance.
Conduct
Everyone taking part in chapter activities agrees to the INFORMS Code of Conduct.

Questions

For anyone new to the chapter.

Do I need to study operations research to join?

No. Anyone at Northeastern can join, in any program or year.

Does it cost anything?

No. Chapter membership is free, and you don't need to be an INFORMS member to join.

How do I join?

Fill in the membership form. You'll get event announcements and invitations.

Can I help run the chapter?

Yes. Several board roles are open this year; see Open Positions above, or write to informsnu@gmail.com.

Where do I hear about events?

On this page under Events, and on the chapter's LinkedIn and Instagram.

Join the chapter

It's free and takes a minute. Fill in the form and you'll hear about every event. If you'd like to help run things, say so on the form or email us.
